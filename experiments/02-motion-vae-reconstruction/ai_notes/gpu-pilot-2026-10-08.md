The zero control has low overall error because most valid queries are static, while its foreground error is extremely poor; aggregate point error alone would favor the wrong supervision.

Tracker decoded-to-GT error can improve through clipping and smoothing. This does not establish improved raw tracking. Label errors and codec errors are vector sums; their scalar EPEs are not additive. Native rates differ.
